Say a bigger job shows up with the same title and the same pay. Your first instinct is probably no.

I took one like that. It meant more areas to own and a lot more responsibility, and I caught myself waiting for the part that would make it sound like a promotion. It never came. Saying yes felt a little exposed, because a title is what other people can see. But I had gotten comfortable in my role, and this was the first thing in a while that made me want to learn something.

Once I moved, my old role was open, and someone on my former team picked up the calls I used to make and ran with them.

Three years later I lead a much larger team and I am still learning. The promotion is still a promise, though I have skills now that I did not have before.

Before you decide on a bigger job with no bigger title, ask what you would learn and who could step up behind you. Then look at what it does for your title.
