A problem lands on your desk marked urgent. Before you fix anything, there is one thing you do not know about it yet.

The pull to start solving is strong, and it feels responsible. A leader I coach felt it when a stalled approval landed on their desk, then stopped and asked the person who brought it, "When did you first notice this?"

The answer was a few weeks. The leader almost said, "Why am I only hearing this now?", which is a fair reaction, and then saw that the number mattered more than the fix.

I know the sting of being the last to hear. What I keep seeing is that the gap between "I noticed it" and "I told you" is what urgency is costing the team. It is information, not an accusation, so listen for the date and ask what got in the way of telling you sooner.

Asking will not shorten the wait on its own. How you take the answer decides whether the next one comes sooner, so thank them for telling you at all.

The problem was urgent when it reached you. The number shows how long it had already been sitting there.
