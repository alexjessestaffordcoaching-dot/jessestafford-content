## Shared Elements

**Core situation:** A leader stands in front of his team after a decision he does not personally like, and has to choose between faking enthusiasm, venting his own frustration, or holding a steady line of real support without pretending he liked the outcome.
**Context box text:** LIKING A DECISION AND SUPPORTING IT ARE DIFFERENT JOBS
**Emotional tone:** Calm and grounded — this is a difficult-conversation, composure-under-stress post. Jesse's expression is steady and plain, not smiling, not grim. Present, not performing.

## Concept 1 — The System

**Visual metaphor:** A dual-gauge signal panel, two separate instrument dials mounted side by side, each reading something different. One gauge is labeled FEELING, one is labeled BACKING. The needles point in different directions, one low, one high, showing they are two different measurements, not one.
**First-second read:** Two gauges, two different readings, on the same panel. Before reading the context box, the viewer understands: these are being measured separately, and they do not have to match.
**Compositional approach:** Jesse stands slightly right of center, close to the panel but not touching it, weight settled, looking at the gauges rather than operating them. The panel is scaled large enough to read both dials clearly. Environment is organized around his position, clarity and decision framing, not peripheral or small.

### Full Expanded Prompt

COMPOSITION — most important: Wide shot. A dual-gauge instrument panel fills the right half of the frame, mounted upright, roughly chest height, with two distinct circular gauges side by side, needles pointing in different directions. Jesse stands in the left-center of the frame, angled three-quarters toward the panel, close enough to be reading it, hands relaxed at his sides, not touching the panel. His scale is roughly equal to the panel's height, positioned deliberately rather than dwarfed by it. Generous negative space above both Jesse and the panel.

Art style spec: Crisp, uniform charcoal ink outlines (#333333), medium line weight throughout. Comic book precision, not sterile flat-art, not heavy superhero cross-hatching. Lines should suggest a technical patent drawing or a well-crafted indie comic panel. Loose, organic watercolor fills, visibly hand-painted. Color intentionally bleeds slightly outside the ink lines or leaves small white gaps at boundaries. Wet-edge textures and transparency variations are required, these are features, not errors. No digital gradients. No glow effects. No perfectly aligned fills. Background: clean white paper texture (#ffffff) with steampunk mechanical elements, objects have exposed mechanics, rendered in charcoal linework as abstract environmental texture. Mechanical background elements suggest systems and structure without being literal or dominant. 16:9 aspect ratio, single panel.

Color palette: Primary watercolor wash: #100299 (deep blue). Canvas and negative space: #ffffff. Linework and shadows: #333333. Shadows are a semi-transparent charcoal watercolor wash, never solid black fill. Supporting colors are allowed for depth and warmth but must not compete with the blue/white/charcoal triad.

Primary character: Man in his early 40s. Short buzz-cut hair. Neatly trimmed short beard and mustache. Brown eyes. V-neck long sleeve shirt in deep blue (#100299) watercolor wash. Charcoal pants. Relaxed, unposed, never stiff or formal. Standing with weight settled evenly, chin level, gaze directed at the gauges with quiet attention. Expression calm and steady, mouth closed and neutral, no smile, no frown.

Scene description: The instrument panel is mounted on a simple exposed-mechanism frame, visible gears and linkages at its edges suggesting it is part of a larger system just out of frame. The left gauge (FEELING) needle sits low. The right gauge (BACKING) needle sits high. Both gauges are rendered with the same clean linework so neither reads as more important than the other, only different. The steampunk background linework is sparse and abstract behind Jesse, small exposed-gear details at the edges of the panel, not filling the frame. Negative space above Jesse's head and to the left is intentional and open.

Context box: Uppercase sans-serif text inside a solid white (#ffffff) rectangle with a sharp charcoal (#333333) border. Placed at the extreme bottom of the panel. Text: LIKING A DECISION AND SUPPORTING IT ARE DIFFERENT JOBS

Tone guardrails: This image should feel like two people talking through a real problem in plain language. Not a motivational poster. Not corporate (no handshakes, whiteboards, or team huddles). Not inspirational abstraction. Grounded, human, and specific. The mechanical background supports the scene, it does not compete with it.

## Concept 3 — The Reframe

**Unexpected angle:** The absent element. A theatrical smiling mask hangs unused on a simple wall hook beside Jesse, plainly visible, but he is not wearing it and is not reaching for it. The unused mask stands in for the forced-cheer option he is choosing not to take.
**First-second read:** A man standing near a smiling mask on a hook, not wearing it, own expression plain and steady. Before reading the context box, the viewer understands: there was a performed version of this available, and he set it aside.
**Why it works:** It makes the post's central choice, fake positivity versus honest support, visible as a physical decision rather than an abstract idea, without needing a caption to explain it.
