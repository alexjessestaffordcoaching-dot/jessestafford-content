Your boss and the company is not asking you to be happy about a layoff or a shrinking raise pool. They are asking whether you will stand behind the decision once you walk out of the room.

I used to blend those two questions into one. Fake a smile I did not feel, or let my own frustration leak out and tell the team it was unfair. Both left the same residue: people stopped trusting what came out of my mouth the next time it mattered.

Liking a decision and supporting it are two different jobs. You are allowed to not love one. You cannot fake one and quietly undercut the other.

Are you being honest about how you feel, or honest about whether you actually back it? If you want a thinking partner for the next one of these, I'm around.
