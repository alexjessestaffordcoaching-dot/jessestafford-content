# Image Prompts — Vera

**Date:** 2026-06-16
**Post:** How to Stop Thinking About Work After Hours (What Actually Works)
**Core visual tension:** Open loops running in the mind vs. the relief of writing things down

---

## Concept 1: The System

**Prompt:**
A close-up, top-down view of a plain open notebook on a desk, a single pen resting across it. Around the notebook, faint translucent speech bubbles or looping circular arrows float in the air — representing unfinished thoughts circling. The bubbles are slightly blurred, soft-edged. The notebook is crisp and grounded. Soft natural light. Muted tones, mostly gray and white, with the notebook as the warmest element in the frame. No text. Editorial, clean.

**What this shows:** The mechanism — the brain's background loops, and the notebook as the place they land.

---

## Concept 2: The Room

**Prompt:**
A manager or professional seated at a kitchen table, dinner plate in front of them, phone face-down beside it. Their gaze is slightly unfocused, directed toward the middle distance, not the food. One hand rests loosely on the table. Evening light through a window, warm but slightly dimming. The scene is quiet and slightly lonely — not distressed, just absent. No text, no devices open, no visible work items. The tension is entirely in the body language and the unfocused gaze. Cinematic, candid-style.

**What this shows:** The emotional core — dinner at the table but mentally still at work. The moment before the fix.

---

## Concept 3: The Reframe

**Prompt:**
A clean desk at end of day. A closed laptop. Next to it, a small open notebook with a few handwritten lines — nothing legible, just the presence of captured items. The notebook is closed partway through, as if just used. Soft late-afternoon or early evening light through a window. The desk is tidy but lived-in — not sterile. A coffee cup off to one side. The overall feeling is: done for the day, permission granted. Warm tones. No phone visible. No screens on. Calm. Documentary-style still life.

**What this shows:** The transformation — the moment the laptop closes with everything written down. Permission to stop.

---

## Usage notes
- The System works best as a featured image for Medium (conceptual, clean, draws the eye)
- The Room works best for LinkedIn (emotional resonance, scrollable)
- The Reframe works best for Blogger and Instagram (aspirational, tangible outcome)
