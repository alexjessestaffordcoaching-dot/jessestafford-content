The leaders I work with who burn their teams out the fastest are not lazy or checked out. They are the most driven people in the room. They add meetings, add asks, add urgency. And they genuinely believe this is what leadership looks like.

I was mentoring a manager who was frustrated that his team was not moving fast enough. He had added check-ins, more requests, more pressure. Nothing was working.

When I asked him what the actual business need was if things stayed at their current pace, he went quiet. He had never asked the question.

There is a difference between applying urgency and evaluating whether urgency is needed. The first assumes more pressure is always the right answer. The second starts from the work: is there a real business need right now, or is the team already moving in the right direction?

A boat still pointed the right way doesn't need you rowing harder. It needs you to check the compass.

What if the leadership behavior you are most proud of is also the reason your team cannot catch up?
