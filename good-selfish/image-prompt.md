## Shared Elements

**Core situation:** A leader is publicly thanked for mentoring work that actually started from a selfish motive — he was sick of bad interviews — and realizes the guilt he's supposed to feel doesn't apply, because the work produced something real for someone else.

**Context box text:** THE MOTIVE WAS SELFISH. THE OUTCOME WASN'T.

**Emotional tone:** Warm and open. Jesse's expression is a quiet, genuine recognition — he may be smiling slightly. This is permission-to-exhale content, not strategy or accountability content.

## Concept 1 — The Evidence

**The artifact:** A worn stack of interview prep note cards on Jesse's desk, years of them, each marked up in his own handwriting — the unglamorous, repeated work behind the public thank-you. Beside the stack, a single handwritten thank-you note, recently opened.

**Setting:** Jesse's home office or den, evening, desk lamp on.

**First-second read:** A thick, well-used stack of note cards next to one small, recent thank-you note. Years of quiet work sit under one moment of recognition.

**Compositional approach:** Jesse close and deliberate, seated at the desk, the stack and the note positioned at human scale in the foreground — the environment organized around his position, the posture of someone taking in what the stack actually represents.

### Full Expanded Prompt

**COMPOSITION — most important:**
Medium-close shot, 16:9, at desk height. A wooden desk runs across the lower two-thirds of the frame. Jesse sits behind the desk, right of center, one hand resting lightly on top of the worn stack, looking down at the new note. His seated scale fills roughly half the frame height. A desk lamp with a visible articulated arm stands at the left edge of the desk, casting warm light across the surface. Negative space occupies the upper-right corner.

**MECHANICAL ENVIRONMENT — second most important:**

No digital gradients. No glow effects. No perfectly aligned fills. Base canvas: clean white paper texture (#ffffff). Single panel.

**Aspect ratio:** 16:9

**Color palette:** Primary watercolor wash: #100299 (deep blue). Canvas and negative space: #ffffff. Linework and shadows: #333333. Shadows are a semi-transparent charcoal watercolor wash — never solid black fill. The charcoal schematic layer stays unpainted linework. The desk wood carries a warm amber wash. The lamp light carries a soft warm cream glow rendered as watercolor wash, not a digital glow effect.

Relaxed, unposed — never stiff or formal. Seated at the desk, one hand resting on the worn stack, looking down at the new note. ## Concept 3 — The Reframe

**Unexpected angle:** Environmental inversion, built around a real mechanism. Jesse works an old hand-crank water pump at a community garden, filling his own watering can — but a visible linked pipe running under a low garden wall simultaneously fills a second trough on the other side, where someone else's plot sits. One motion, two outputs.

**Setting:** A community garden with a shared hand-crank pump station.

**First-second read:** A man filling his own watering can at a hand pump. A second pipe, visibly linked to the same pump, is filling a trough on the other side of a low wall at the same time. His effort is going two directions at once.

**Why it works:** The reader expects self-interested effort to serve only the person doing it. Showing the same crank motion physically filling something on the other side of the wall — without Jesse doing anything extra — makes the article's actual claim (the benefit lands somewhere else, without subtracting from the motive) visible as a mechanism instead of an argument.

On the near side of that wall, a visible pipe branches off the pump's base and runs along the wall's foot, emerging on the far side into a small trough that's visibly filling — the trough sits in the lower-left of the frame, smaller and more distant than Jesse and the pump.
