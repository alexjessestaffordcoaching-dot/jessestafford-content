I started mentoring university students because I kept getting terrible interviews.

That is not the selfless origin story. It is the opposite.

The motivation was mine. Years later, I was publicly recognized for the support I give those students.

The outcome was real and the motivation was selfish. Those two things do not cancel each other out.

What selfish reason is driving your best leadership right now?
