A manager used AI to score the sentiment of their team's 1-on-1s. Not notes. A grade on how positive you sounded.

Nobody asked if that felt okay to sit through, and that is the part that stays with you.

Consent still matters. It is just not the same thing as honesty. People sign off on a recording, then quietly say less, not more, the second they remember it exists.

I watched the same thing happen recording coaching calls years ago. Same consent. Shorter, safer answers, like the room had gotten a little colder.
