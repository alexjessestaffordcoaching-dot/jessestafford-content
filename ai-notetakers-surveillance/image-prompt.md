## Shared Elements

**Core situation:** A small AI notetaker device sits on the table in a 1-on-1, and the moment someone notices it is there, what they say starts to shrink.

**Context box text:** CONSENT IS NOT THE SAME AS HONESTY

**Emotional tone:** Calm and grounded. This is a difficult-conversation, composure-under-scrutiny topic, not a warm connection topic or a pure strategy topic. Jesse's expression across concepts is quietly attentive and a little troubled, someone noticing something he does not like, not alarmed and not performing concern.

## Concept 1 — The Evidence

**The artifact:** A palm-sized AI meeting recorder puck, the kind that sits on a conference table and glows while it listens. This is the literal object the post is about, not a metaphor for it.
**Setting:** A small glass-walled 1-on-1 meeting room, the kind most offices have one of, with two chairs and a narrow table.
**First-second read:** A man alone in a meeting room reaching toward a small glowing device on the table, mid-decision about whether to turn it off.
**Compositional approach:** Jesse close to the table, deliberate, reaching toward the device rather than posed beside it. Clarity/decision positioning: he is the one acting on the object, the room organized around that one small gesture.

### Full Expanded Prompt

**COMPOSITION — most important:** 16:9 single panel. A small glass-walled 1-on-1 meeting room, seen at a slight angle so both the glass wall and the interior table are visible. The narrow table runs diagonally from lower-left to mid-frame. Jesse sits in the near chair, leaning forward, right hand extended toward a small puck-shaped device sitting on the table's center, his fingers just short of touching it. The second chair, empty, sits across the table in the middle-right of the frame. Jesse occupies the lower-left third at human scale; the device is small, roughly the size of his fist, positioned at the exact center of the table where both chairs would naturally look. Glass wall and hallway beyond fill the upper-right background, softly rendered, empty of people.

**MECHANICAL ENVIRONMENT — second most important:** The puck device: matte charcoal-gray body, a thin ring collar around its upper rim that is lit with a visible glow, a small perforated microphone grille pattern on its top face. A yellow legal pad sits beside it, spiral-bound, blank pages visible and explicitly unlettered. The meeting room door has a visible lever handle and hinge line on the glass frame. Charcoal schematic linework occupies the upper-left corner of the frame and the wall plane above the glass partition, rendered as blueprint-style annotation of the device's ring-light circuit and a cutaway of its internal microphone diaphragm. The S mark is worked into this schematic in the upper-left corner, rendered as a stamped component label within the linework, in charcoal, not painted.

**Art style spec:** Crisp, uniform charcoal ink outlines (#333333), medium line weight throughout. Comic book precision — not sterile flat-art, not heavy superhero cross-hatching. Lines should suggest a technical patent drawing or a well-crafted indie comic panel. Loose, organic watercolor fills — visibly hand-painted. Color intentionally bleeds slightly outside the ink lines or leaves small white gaps at boundaries. Wet-edge textures and transparency variations are required — these are features, not errors. No digital gradients. No glow effects. No perfectly aligned fills. Base canvas: clean white paper texture (#ffffff). Single panel.

**Aspect ratio:** 16:9

**Color palette:** Primary watercolor wash: #100299 (deep blue). Canvas and negative space: #ffffff. Linework and shadows: #333333. Shadows are a semi-transparent charcoal watercolor wash, never solid black fill. The charcoal schematic layer stays unpainted linework. Supporting colors are allowed for depth and warmth but must not compete with the blue/white/charcoal triad. The device's ring-light glow uses a soft warm amber wash as a supporting color, kept small and contained to the collar itself.

**Primary character:** Man in his early 40s. Short buzz-cut hair. Neatly trimmed short beard and mustache. Brown eyes. V-neck long sleeve shirt in deep blue (#100299) watercolor wash. Charcoal pants. Relaxed, unposed. Leaning forward from the near chair, right arm extended toward the device, weight forward on his forearm on the table, expression quietly attentive, a little troubled, focused entirely on the device rather than the camera.

**Scene description:** The meeting room is small, plain, and recognizable, the kind of glass-box room used for one-on-ones in almost any office. The table is narrow, wood-toned watercolor wash, with light scuffing texture. Beside the legal pad, a plain ceramic mug sits with no visible text or logo.
