Some managers are already using AI to score the sentiment of their team's 1-on-1s. Not action items. Whether the person sounded positive or negative.

Nobody asked the direct reports if that felt okay. The consent form probably just said meetings may be recorded.

I ran into a version of this years ago, recording client calls for coach training. Every client agreed to it. The calls still got shorter and less honest the moment they remembered the recorder was there.

Consent is not the same as honesty. A signature does not make someone forget there is a second audience in the room.
