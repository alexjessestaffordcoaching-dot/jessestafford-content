LinkedIn Post 2: What Words Change How Your Team Listens?

Platform: LinkedIn | Target: 3-5 paragraphs, 30-second read Status: Approved.

When I started watching my own language, something unexpected happened. I started hearing my team's words differently too.

A team member told me he would try to have the analysis ready by Thursday. Before I ran the vocabulary experiment, I would have let that pass. After several weeks of paying attention to word choices, I heard what "try" was doing. It was building in an exit before the conversation ended.

"Try" signals someone is already preparing for not finishing. "I will" signals something different. It is not about confidence or attitude. It is about which word reflects what they actually intend to do.

I stopped correcting it directly. I started asking instead: "What would need to be true for you to say 'I will have this ready by Thursday'?" That question makes the vague commitment specific. It surfaces the actual constraint.

Changing your language does not just change how you speak. It changes what you hear.
