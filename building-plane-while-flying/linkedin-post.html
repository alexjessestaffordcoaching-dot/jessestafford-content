Not having all the answers is not the same as not having a direction.

I see this confusion come up regularly during transformation. They treat uncertainty about the details as a reason to go silent about the direction. The team reads that silence as uncertainty about everything.

You know where you are going. You know why the change is happening. You know what success looks like. Those things are clear even when the exact path is not.

Lead with what you know. Acknowledge what is still being figured out. Then move.

The team does not need the complete plan. They need to see that you can function without it.

Are you confusing not having the details with not having the direction?
