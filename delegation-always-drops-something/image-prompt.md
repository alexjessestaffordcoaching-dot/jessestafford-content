## Shared Elements

**Core situation:** A leader hands off work and feels relief, not realizing the delegation has cascaded down the chain until something quietly dropped at the bottom — discovered only when they trace it all the way down.

**Context box text:** SOMETHING ALWAYS FALLS OUT THE BOTTOM

**Emotional tone:** Direct and focused. Jesse's expression is calm and clear-eyed throughout — the post acknowledges an uncomfortable truth without dramatizing it. The expression of a leader paying close attention to something they had been missing.

**Visual metaphor:** A descending chain of relay nodes (interconnected schematic boxes and pipes) representing the delegation chain. Work passes from node to node. The leak at the far right is what the viewer sees that Jesse cannot.

**Approach:** Ironic juxtaposition — the system appears to work at every step visible to the leader. The failure only exists where he cannot see.
