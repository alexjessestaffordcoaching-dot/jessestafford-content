When you delegate something, you do not free up capacity. You move it.

The person you hand work to is already busy. They absorb the task or pass it further down. The chain continues until it hits someone with no one left to pass to. At that point, something gets dropped or someone works overtime. The thing that gets dropped may not have been what you would have chosen.

Why? Because no one along the chain had the authority to say "I do not have room for this."

I found out about a cascade in my own team through a skip-level conversation three months after I made the original handoff. Something had quietly fallen out the bottom. Nobody surfaced it because nobody knew how to without it feeling like a complaint about their manager.

If you manage managers: when did you last follow a piece of work all the way to where it actually landed?
