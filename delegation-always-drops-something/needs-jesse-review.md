**Exact source text (from story-69):**
> "Delegation will always lead to something not getting done.
>

>
> Backstory, was doing a skip level one on one. Team member was trying to figure out how to get all their work done after their manager had delegated something to them. Funny enough their manager told them what caused the work to get delegated and it was something I had delegated to them.

**What was composited:** The specific tasks were invented per your instruction. The composite used:

- The person at the end of the chain: an engineer already behind on a documentation backlog
- What dropped: the documentation backlog

The skip-level conversation structure is from Story 69 verbatim — Jesse asking what picked up, finding out about the weekly reports, then asking what was set aside.
