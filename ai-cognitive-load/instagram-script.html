CAPTION

One of my teams finished its sprint work by Wednesday. By Friday, the two strongest engineers looked worn down.

Not from long hours. The small tickets and boilerplate AI now handles were also the recovery time between hard problems. AI removed the rest, not the work.

Everyone said they felt faster, and they were right. That is exactly why the strain was so hard to place: the individual signals all look good, and the team-level ones (rework, churn, people going quiet before Monday) are where it shows.
