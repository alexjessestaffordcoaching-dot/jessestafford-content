Years ago my team of five logged every hour, focused or not, for months. Past 4.5 focused hours a day, burnout showed up.

About 4.5 hours was the peak, and they were happiest closer to 3.5. By the end of the days that ran past 4.5, I could see it in the team. The rest went to helping each other, learning, meetings and hallway conversations. All still work.

That was before we used AI tools. Now AI clears out much of what filled those hours, and it is easy to expect a full day of deep thinking from people who suddenly have the room. On my teams the focused hours usually stretch to fill it, and nobody decided that.

A full day at work was never a full day of deep thinking. In planning I have started treating that part of the day as its own budget. We found our number by counting focused and unfocused hours on one team of five in a corporate office, so yours may be different.

How many focused hours does your team really have in a day?

If you want a thinking partner for working that number out, I'm around.
