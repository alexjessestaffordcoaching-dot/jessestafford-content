I once emailed my manager what I was going to do and gave them one hour to stop me.

I had a manager who never scheduled 1-on-1s unless they needed something from me. They were not cancelled. They did not exist.

A cancelled 1-on-1 says less about you than you think. A pattern says more about the manager. Either they are in a great place and aligned with you, or they are actively disengaged. From the outside it looks the same.

Send three lines: what you are working on, what you plan to do next, what you need. If the 1-on-1 is not happening, that note is your 1-on-1.
