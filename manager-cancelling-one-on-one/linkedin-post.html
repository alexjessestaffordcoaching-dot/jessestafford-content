I had a manager who never scheduled 1-on-1s unless they needed something from me.

They were not cancelled. They did not exist. So I started emailing my priorities and the actions I would take. Once, for a team announcement, I wrote that I was going to send it and they had one hour to stop me.

That habit taught me what a missing 1-on-1 costs. The manager loses the meeting where small problems show up while they are still small. The direct report loses the place their work gets seen.

When I am the one moving a 1-on-1, I have noticed people create artificial worry, and work starts to drift a little.

Is your calendar protecting your 1-on-1s, or just the meetings that are easier to defend?
