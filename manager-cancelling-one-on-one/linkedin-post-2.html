"No worries, enjoy the free time." It is the easiest reply to a cancelled 1-on-1, and I used to type it too.

A third cancellation comes in, I send that reply, and nothing on the calendar changes. It felt like a relief, because nobody had to have an awkward conversation. In the past I would have read the quiet as my work not being a priority. Today I assume vacation or a fire drill. Either way, the reply kept the moment easy and told me nothing.

A manager who is fine with no 1-on-1s is either in a great place and sees us as aligned, or is disengaged. From the outside those look the same.

So now I reply with two times that week. A cancellation that turns into a new time is just scheduling. One that evaporates is a pattern, and then I know which conversation I am in.

If the next cancellation gets that reply, what comes back is either a new time or silence, and both tell you something.
