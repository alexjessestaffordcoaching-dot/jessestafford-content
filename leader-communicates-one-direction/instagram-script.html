Caption:

A compliance leader once opened our first meeting with, "Can you tell me how a manager from the studio side is going to make compliance work better?" Fair question. I had shown up with an agenda and no relationship.

You get fluent at talking up to your manager and down to your team. The direction nobody practices is sideways, to the people who owe you nothing and control half your project.

The fix is not being more assertive. It is starting at your own level, learning what the other person is actually accountable for, and framing your work in their terms before you ask for anything.

Getting things done across an org chart you do not control is the exact pattern I coach through. Link in bio if you want to talk it through.
