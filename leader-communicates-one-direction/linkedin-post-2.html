Before hello, the compliance leader asked me, "Can you tell me how a manager from the studio side is going to help make compliance work better?" It was a fair question, and my face probably showed that I did not have a good answer yet, because I had not built a relationship with that team.

So I paused, introduced myself, and said what I had been asked to help with. Then I asked about their work instead of mine: how tasks move through their team, and where they jam when another department gets involved.

They knew more about it than I did, and you cannot walk in and tell people what their job is. Ask how their work moves, where it jams, and what they are measured on. Then ask for what you need in their words.

Starting with questions when you walked in with an ask feels like giving up your time, and one meeting does not prove it works everywhere.

That was the moment I saw I had not done enough relationship building yet.
