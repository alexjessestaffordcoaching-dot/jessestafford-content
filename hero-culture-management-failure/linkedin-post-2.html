Everyone says document it, pair on it, rotate it. Few people say what to tell the engineer who has been the only one who knows.

That person may like the arrangement. Being the only one feels like job security, and it makes their manager look good, so nothing on their side pushes for change.

What I do is sit <name> down. I start with where the team and the company are going, then I talk about their career. I say, plainly and softly, that staying tied to this one area is not where their next step is.

After that the work moves slowly. Someone sits in, takes the first piece, and the person starts on something new.

If you are stuck because the person you would hand the work to is the person who benefits from keeping it, that is where coaching helps.
