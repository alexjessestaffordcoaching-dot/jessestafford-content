## Story Used

**Why it was selected:** Riley identified this as the strongest direct match in the story bank for "creating change" rather than "announcing it" — it's a concrete example of Jesse's team killing an old mechanism (a 100+ person email blast) entirely and replacing it with a targeted process, rather than just telling the compliance group to "communicate better." The result (8 hours/day recovered, the company's first-ever clean regulatory audit) is proof the change actually stuck.

**Exact text used as the anchor (Medium's "What Creating It Actually Looks Like" section):**
> "Compliance Group Communication: This involved working with the company's compliance organization, which I am not a direct member of. They moved their practices from sending a bulk email to 100+ people with every question to a process using project tracking tools and directing messages to the specific people who needed to respond. In the end, this removed 8 hours of responding to emails every day, allowing a whole person's effort to be dedicated to other tasks. It also led to the first time in company history that there were no complaints from regulatory agencies."
