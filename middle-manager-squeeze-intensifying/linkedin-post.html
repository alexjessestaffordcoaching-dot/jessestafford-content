There is a rule I keep coming back to as a manager: I can vent to my peers and to my boss. I cannot vent down.

Recently, this rule mattered when I had to stand in front of my team and explain a decision I did not make. My job was to deliver it as the right way forward. My view on the timing was not part of the job.

Holding back was annoying. It can feel like pretending. But there is a difference between what your team needs to hear and what you are carrying. Venting down hands them something they cannot act on. That is on you, not the decision.

Tell your boss where the pressure is sitting. Tell them what the team's reaction has been and where you are holding the line. They have been there. They can actually do something with it. The vent goes up. The explanation goes down.

Where are you venting that you should not be?
