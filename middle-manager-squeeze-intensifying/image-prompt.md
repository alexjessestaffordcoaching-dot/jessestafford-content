# Image Prompts — Vera

**Date:** 2026-10-30
**Article:** Should You Stay in Management When the Pressure Never Lets Up?
**Brand colors:** Primary #100299 (deep blue), Background #ffffff, Accent #333333

---

## Concept 1 — Medium Header / Blogger Header

**Scene:** A person standing alone at the front of a conference table, facing empty chairs. The room is lit from above with flat corporate light. The person is mid-turn, as if they are about to speak but have not yet started. Their posture is straight, composed — not defeated. A stack of papers or a single document sits on the table in front of them.

**Mood:** Quiet authority before a hard conversation. Composure in the middle.

**Palette:** Blue-white office light. Charcoal suit or dark neutral tones. The empty chairs suggest the team has yet to arrive — the moment before the explaining begins.

**No text in image.**

**Use for:** Medium header (1400×787px), Blogger header.

---

## Concept 2 — LinkedIn Header

**Scene:** Two columns on a torn piece of paper or a legal pad page. Left column header: "WHAT I CAN AFFECT." Right column header: "WHAT I CAN ONLY INFLUENCE." The left side has a few items written; the right side has a single entry with a line drawn through it. A pen rests on the paper. Background is clean, minimal.

**Mood:** Clarity through sorting. Something being let go.

**Palette:** White legal pad paper, dark ink, brand blue (#100299) as a subtle background wash or desk surface.

**No text in image (except the column headers, which are part of the visual prop).**

**Use for:** LinkedIn post header (1200×628px).

---

## Concept 3 — Alternative Medium / Feature Image

**Scene:** A person standing at a window in a high-rise building, looking out at the city below. From behind. Posture is upright and still. The city lights are visible but out of focus. The person's reflection shows faint detail in the glass. One hand rests against the frame. 

**Mood:** The alone-in-the-middle moment. Thinking it through. Not overwhelmed — present.

**Palette:** Dusk or early evening light. City blues and oranges through the glass. The figure in dark tones. Brand blue (#100299) can echo in the glass reflection or city lights.

**No text in image.**

**Use for:** Alternate Medium header or feature image variant.

---

## Notes for art direction

- All three concepts avoid showing the face of the subject. This makes the figure identifiable to the reader, not a specific person.
- No signs of distress in any image. The emotional tone is steady composure in the face of sustained pressure — not burnout imagery.
- Source Sans Pro for any overlay typography if used by the publisher. Do not add text to the prompts above.
- For AI image generation: specify "photorealistic, corporate environment, cinematic composition, no text" for all three prompts.
