Telling leadership what AI is really doing for your team does not make you the skeptic. The order you say it in does.

Open with the team's problem and the room hears a position on AI. Open with what is true about the gain and the rest lands as more information, though not every room takes it that way.

Same facts, different first sentence: "It is not really working for the team" makes the room go quiet, and "Yes, the writing got faster" lands as information.

I check what I say against one test. If my boss only remembers the first thirty seconds, is that what I need them to know?

If you know the real picture and stay quiet because you do not want to sound negative, I help leaders find the first sentence to say.
