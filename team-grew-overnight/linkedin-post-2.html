Soon after my team grew, someone asked if I was still sending the weekly update to our internal customers. I had stopped days earlier and had not told anyone, and I felt a little embarrassed that they had been waiting on it.

They were being reasonable. They could see I was stretched, and they assumed I was still handling the things I was no longer handling. Nobody had told them otherwise.

When nobody says it, the work is not handed off, it just stops, and the people who depend on it quietly stop hearing what the team is doing.

These days when my scope changes, I say it before anyone has to ask: "I am not covering the customer update anymore, and here is who has it." That does not make every handoff easy, but nobody is left waiting on something that is not coming.

What have you stopped doing that your team still thinks you are doing?

If you want a thinking partner for sorting out what to hand off, I am around.
