## Shared Elements

**Core situation:** After his team quadrupled almost overnight, Jesse realized the invisible work he had done for two years — tending internal-customer relationships, translating the team's work into business language, keeping the team visible — and every reason it mattered lived only in his head, so when he could no longer do it, it did not get handed off; it just stopped.

**Context box text:** THEY KNEW THE STEPS. NOT WHY THEY MATTERED.

**Emotional tone:** Calm and grounded. Jesse is a leader reflecting on a gap he did not see coming — clear-eyed, composed, not performing guilt and not catastrophizing. His expression is steady with a trace of hard-won recognition.

## Concept 1 — The Evidence

**The artifact:** A rotary card index (Rolodex-style desk file) sitting on Jesse's desk. The front two-thirds of its cards are dense with handwriting — names, contacts, notes built over years. The back third are blank. It fits because the story is about knowledge that was transferable only if it got written down, and most of it never did: the informal work "that nobody has put in writing."
**Setting:** Jesse's desk in a quiet open-plan office, early morning, before anyone else is in.
**First-second read:** A man at his desk has turned a card file all the way around to the part that was never filled in.
**Compositional approach:** The card index sits center-right on the desk at human scale; Jesse is close and deliberate, one hand on the advance wheel, looking down at the blank cards. Environment organized around his position.

### Full Expanded Prompt

**COMPOSITION — most important:**
Medium shot, 16:9. A single office desk runs across the lower half of the frame. Jesse sits close behind the desk, leaning in, his right hand resting on the index's thumb-wheel, his eyes down on the empty cards. To the left on the same desk stand three upright ring binders, closed, spines facing out and unlettered. The rest of the desk is clear. Behind Jesse is a plain office wall; the upper-left quarter of the frame is open negative space. Early-morning light, no other people, empty desks suggested softly in the far background.

**MECHANICAL ENVIRONMENT — second most important:**
Objects and their working mechanism: (1) the rotary card index — visible central spindle, knurled thumb-wheel on its right side mid-rotation, hinged lid arm and pivot, the card stack fanned on the spindle; the front cards thick with handwriting, the presented rear cards bare white. (3) A single articulated monitor arm at the desk's left edge, folded, its spring and pivot joints exposed. An older line cook has just handed Jesse a single laminated card and turned back to the pass. Jesse holds the card, watching the cook walk away, because the card gives him the steps but not which tickets matter when the rail fills.
**Setting:** The line of a working commercial kitchen — flat-top range, ticket rail, pass.
**First-second read:** A cook has been left holding an instruction card while the person who actually knows how the line runs walks off.
**Why it works:** Nobody would hand off a kitchen station with a steps-only card — yet that is exactly how the invisible leadership work got handed off, and the kitchen makes the gap obvious at a glance.
