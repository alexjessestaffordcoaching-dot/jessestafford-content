Working with QA feels like a constant negotiation. Test counts, deadline pressure, and nobody who can tell you what would actually hurt customers if it shipped.

Your first assumption is that QA is the problem. The communication gap. The risk language they are not using.

But QA gives you what you ask for. If your status question is "how many test cases are left?" that is what they prepare for.

Ask "what concerns you most about this release?" and you get a different kind of answer. It takes a few sprints to build. But it comes.

If you changed the question you ask QA, what did you start hearing that you were not hearing before?
