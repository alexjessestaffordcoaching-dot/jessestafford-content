## Concept 1: The System

**What it represents:** The measurement system that drives QA behavior — dashboards full of defect counts that answer no real question. The leader is surrounded by numbers that don't add up to what they need to know.

**Mood:** Detached. The numbers are visible but the meaning is missing.

**AI image prompt:**
A male engineering leader in his 40s sits alone at a large conference table, looking at an open laptop. The screen shows rows of numbers and charts. His expression is focused but unsatisfied — he is looking for an answer the screen cannot give. Clean office environment. Soft side lighting. Muted blues and grays. Realistic, editorial style. No text in the image.

## Concept 2: The Room

**What it represents:** The sprint review scene — the moment someone asks about customer risk and the room goes quiet. No one can answer. The spreadsheet comes out. More counts.

**Mood:** Tense, but not hostile. The silence is the point.

**AI image prompt:**
A small software team meeting room. Five people seated around a table — a mix of engineers and a manager. One person is asking a question with open hands; the others are looking at each other or at a laptop screen showing test metrics. No one is speaking. The body language shows uncertainty, not conflict. Natural daylight. Muted, realistic colors. Editorial style. No text in the image.

**Stock photo search terms:**
- "software team meeting quiet moment"
- "engineers in conference room spreadsheet"
- "team meeting tension silent"
- "engineering sprint review"

## Concept 3: The Reframe

**What it represents:** The shift — a different kind of conversation between a leader and a QA lead. Not a status update. A genuine exchange about what matters. The mood is collaborative, not transactional.

**Mood:** Focused, direct, productive. The two people are equals working the same problem.

**AI image prompt:**
Two people standing side by side at a whiteboard or standing near a monitor — a leader and a QA engineer, both looking at the same thing and pointing or gesturing toward it. The body language is open and engaged. They are solving something together. Office environment, natural lighting. Realistic, editorial style. Warm tones. No text in the image.

**Stock photo search terms:**
- "two engineers whiteboard discussion"
- "manager QA engineer collaboration"
- "team working together problem solving"
- "developer and manager reviewing together"
