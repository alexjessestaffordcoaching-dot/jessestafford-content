Three questions work better in 1-on-1s after a significant change than any explanation of the rationale.

Most leaders go into those conversations still in explanation mode. The person across from you is not debating the decision. They are processing what they lost.

Ask what they liked about the way their work used to be measured. Then ask what doing a good job looks like to them under the new goals. Where do they feel like they have the most control right now?

Those questions do not skip the discomfort. They move through it. And the answers tell you what actually needs rebuilding.
