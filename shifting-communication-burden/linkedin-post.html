I have watched leaders spend hours building the perfect status dashboard. Updated every week. Color-coded. Pinned in Slack. Their team is still asking the questions the dashboard answers.

The dashboard is not the problem.

The missing piece is a delivery moment. Something in their team's week that says "this is when you look at this." Without a trigger, the information sits. People are busy. They are not opening your Confluence page because nothing in their day points them to it.

A short email with one update and a link. Thirty seconds in standup. A calendar item that puts the review on their schedule. Those are triggers. That is communication. The dashboard is the filing system. The trigger is the actual work.

If your team is still asking questions your update doc already answers, what is the one thing that would tell them when to open it?
