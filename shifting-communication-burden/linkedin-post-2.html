"Check the thread." That is the reply I get when I ask where a project stands, and what I hear is that finding the answer is now my job.

The thread is long, the answer is buried somewhere in it, and I can feel my day getting smaller as I scroll. The person who sent me there did real work keeping everything in one place, and they meant it as help. For me it turned into the next stretch of my afternoon, reading backwards for one sentence.

Make me read through a long email history to find your update, and you are wasting my time. Send me the update, and you are not. Something like "it slipped a week because of the review, and the thread has the details if you want them" is all I need.

I can only speak to how it lands with me, and I have said "it is in the doc" to my own people. So the next time someone asks where it stands, would you rather send the answer or the place it lives?
