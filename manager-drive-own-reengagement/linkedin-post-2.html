Some days fall apart before lunch. A meeting runs over, the data report I saved the afternoon for gets pushed, and I can feel the day getting away from me.

Then I sit down with someone I coach and we work out how they want to handle a hard conversation with their team. For an hour I am listening and thinking instead of putting out fires, and my shoulders come down. It is the part of the job I like best.

Recently I noticed that it does not matter how bad the rest of the day got if at least an hour of it was good. The meetings are still waiting at four, but I am not carrying all of them.

A lot of days that hour does not show up on its own, so I put it there. Each morning I ask myself what will give me energy, and I make sure something like it is in the day, usually mentoring or coaching someone.

If you have not pinned down the hour in your day that gives you energy yet, that is where I work with leaders in coaching.
