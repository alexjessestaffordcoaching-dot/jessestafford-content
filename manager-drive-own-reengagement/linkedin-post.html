"Managers are supposed to..." is a sentence, not a rule. Most of us finish it with whatever someone else needs from us.

Be available. Attend everything. Absorb it all. Then we call the result duty.

I got to a point where I was putting in three or four hours a day and doing the bare minimum. From the outside, nothing looked wrong.

What changed things was seeing it as a choice. Not a rule I had to obey, but a sentence I could finish myself, starting with which part of the job I actually wanted to do.

What is your "managers are supposed to..." sentence, and who decided it?
