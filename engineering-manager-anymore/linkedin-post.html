Being internal does not mean you have the inside track.

I had two outside offers in hand before I understood what I had missed. I had done every career development thing my one-on-ones had suggested. What I had not done was build relationships on the team I wanted to lead. Nobody there knew what I was capable of. I did not know their work. If the seat had opened that week, I would have been a stranger who happened to have a badge.

By the time a management role gets posted, the people making the decision usually already know who they want. The decision does not happen when the req goes live. It happens in the months before, through the conversations that either happened or did not.

Build those relationships before you need them. Not to impress anyone. Not to signal your ambition. Just to learn what they do and why it matters, so by the time there is a question, you are someone they already know.

Who on the team you want to lead have you talked to this week?

If you want help getting into those conversations before the seat exists, I am around.
