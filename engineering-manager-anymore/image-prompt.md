## Concept 1 — The System

**Visual metaphor:** A massive circular vault-style gear door representing the management seat. Three distinct internal lever mechanisms, etched with simple schematic icons (an upward arrow for growth, an outward arrow for departure, a rotating bracket for reorg), are already engaged and turning the door open from the inside. At the base of the door, off to the side, sits a small mail slot labeled with nothing but a simple envelope icon, where an application would be dropped.
**First-second read:** A huge mechanical door is already turning open, driven by three internal levers, while a small figure near a mail slot at the bottom is completely disconnected from what's making it move.
**Compositional approach:** Jesse is small and peripheral, standing at the mail slot at the lower edge of the frame, having just dropped an envelope through it, looking up and across at the massive gear door that has already begun opening above and to the right of him, entirely independent of his action.

An ornate, important-looking empty chair, detailed with visible mechanical joinery like it belongs to the gear door in Concept 1, sits highlighted at the center-back of the frame under a single warm light. But the actual focus of the image is Jesse in the foreground, off to one side, in a relaxed conversation with a colleague over coffee, neither of them looking at the chair at all.
**First-second read:** An important, spotlighted empty seat sits waiting in the background, but the two people who actually matter to the frame are turned away from it, mid-conversation, off to the side.
**Why it works:** It inverts the instinct to stare at the empty seat and chase it directly. The real work — the thing that actually determines who eventually sits there — is happening in the unremarkable conversation nobody is treating as the moment that matters.
