I used to think "communicate better" counted as feedback. I said some version of it more than once, in more than one review.

Nobody ever changed anything afterward, because I never actually told them what to change.

It sounded like feedback. It had the tone of feedback, the pause, the eye contact. It just did not have a single instruction inside it.

Situation. Behavior. Impact. What would have been better. Four sentences instead of one word, and suddenly there is something a person can actually do differently.

If the feedback you are sitting on right now is one word, communication, strategy, ownership, what is the sentence underneath it that you have not said yet?
