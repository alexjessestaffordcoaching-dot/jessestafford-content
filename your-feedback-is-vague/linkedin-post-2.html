"Be more strategic" is hard to argue with, and that is exactly why it is so comfortable to say. You never have to point at the moment, the words that were said, or what they led to. And you never have to defend that you read it right.

The first time I coached a new manager through their first round of real feedback, the easy version would have taken five seconds and meant nothing to anyone. I am not sure this is the only way to do it, but I gave them a formula, and the result sounded like "Yesterday in our team meeting, you challenged Sam in a combative way, and the others stopped contributing."

The person on the receiving end cannot argue with a category, which means they cannot act on it either. They walk away with a bruise and no instructions.

What would change if someone helped you find the actual moment behind the category you have been using?
