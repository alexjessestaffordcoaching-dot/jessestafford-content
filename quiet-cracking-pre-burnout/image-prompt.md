## Concept 1 — The Evidence

**The artifact:** The attrition-forecasting spreadsheet Jesse describes building during the pandemic — printed pages of trend lines that predicted who would leave, months before it happened. The physical trace this situation leaves is data that saw the risk before any conversation did.
**Setting:** A home office table at night, papers spread out, everyone else in the house asleep.
**First-second read:** A man alone at a table at night, tracing one line among several printed charts with his finger — something is connecting for him that wasn't obvious before.
**Compositional approach:** Jesse close and deliberate, seated directly at the table, the papers arranged in the space directly in front of him — a clarity/decision moment, environment organized around his position.
