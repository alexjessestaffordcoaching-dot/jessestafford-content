The person who always had an opinion answers your question with "Whatever you think," and the project update right after it is on time.

The work side was fine, and a good update lets me skip the harder question. There's a half second where I can feel how easy that is.

Lately I listen for how a reliable person talks to me. The one who used to send updates before the meeting now waits to be asked, the one who drove the conversation says "Fine," and the one with strong views hands me the decision. Each one is polite, and each feels like someone stepping back from the table.

When the meeting is all about the work, the work is all that comes up, and the work is the part that still looks fine. I know what it is like when nobody asks, so when I hear one of these now, I set the project aside and ask about the person. I can't tell you it means burnout every time, and a quiet week can just be a quiet week.

If your 1-on-1s stay on the work and you are hearing "Whatever you think" from someone who used to have a view, that is where I help leaders in coaching.
