There is one question that changes what your team reports to you.

Most managers ask what shipped. I changed this on my own teams and the updates I started getting were different. If asking what shipped is the only question on your list, you will quietly teach people that catching problems before anyone sees them does not count, and the careful ones will stop bringing it up.

Ask what they caught this week instead. It tells your team that the checking is real work. It also hands you the line you need the next time you are reporting up, because you have now heard it in their words.

A good catch looks exactly like a quiet week to everyone outside the room. You are the first person who can change that.

Who on your team caught something this week that their skip-level will never hear about?
