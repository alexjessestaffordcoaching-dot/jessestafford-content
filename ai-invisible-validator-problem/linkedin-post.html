I read a whole contract because I had some extra unallocated moments. A teammate had an AI summary of it instead.

The summary had hallucinations and errors that would have set back our negotiations. Because I read the source, they were not set back. Nobody reacted, because from the outside nothing had happened.

That is the problem with checking AI's work. A good catch looks exactly like a quiet week, and a quiet week is easy for everyone to forget.

Think about the last status update you sent. Managers only know what their team tells them. If you caught it and said nothing, your manager cannot repeat what they never heard.

Make it business impact, not technical challenge: what you caught, what it would have cost, what you did, in one sentence they can pass along with minimal translation. I do not have a perfect system for this yet, and that sentence is the closest I have found.

Who on your team caught something this week that nobody outside the room knows about?
