Early in my career, someone pulled me aside and told me I was too blunt, too direct, and not politically correct enough for the room I was in. I heard it as a verdict on my ability.

Now I work at some of the highest levels in my organization, and that same directness is an asset. I did learn to read a room along the way, and people came to appreciate that I was forthright, but the directness never went away. What changed was who was listening.

When I hear a leader call someone a bad communicator, I wonder who that person has been talking to. Calling it a personality problem is the easy answer, because the other answer is that you may need to change how you deliver. That person may land very differently with a different audience or in a different channel.

If you have already filed someone on your team under "bad communicator," that is exactly the label I work through with leaders in coaching.
