Nobody is naturally good at communicating. They just learned to talk to people who already talk like them, and somewhere along the way that got mistaken for a natural gift.

I have watched leaders write off a strong performer as "difficult" for being terse in meetings or slow on Slack, when that was never actually the problem.

Why?

A quiet, terse person and a checked-out, disengaged one look identical from across the room, so leaders default to the version of communication that is easiest to judge. Confusing an inability to communicate with an inability to perform punishes the wrong person for the wrong reason.

What if the "bad communicator" on your team is actually just waiting on you to deliver information the way they can hear it?
