One of my direct reports had visible reactions to surprise news, and they landed in front of whoever was in the room. I gave feedback more than once, the conversations went well, and two weeks later it happened again.

For a while I kept working on them instead of on when they heard the news. Then I shared news with them ahead of time whenever I could, and let them react with me, privately, before anyone else was involved.

By the next team meeting the reaction was spent and they showed up settled. A reaction has to land somewhere, and I would rather it land with me than in front of the team. I did not ask them to react differently, and I do not know that this fits every pattern.

When hard news reaches your team, where does the first reaction happen: in the room, or with you?
