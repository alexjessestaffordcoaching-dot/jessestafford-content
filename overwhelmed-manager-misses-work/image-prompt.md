## Concept 1 — The Evidence

**The artifact:** A long strip of blank receipt-style paper running from the manager's desk across the floor, standing for an update whose one important line sits at the far end where the manager never reaches.
**Setting:** The manager's cluttered private office.
**First-second read:** An absurdly long note, with a small person holding its far end: the thing that matters is a long way from the person reading.
**Compositional approach:** Jesse small and peripheral in the lower-left at the paper's end, the manager small at the desk on the right; the paper's length is the distance between them.

**COMPOSITION — most important**
Wide shot from the corner of a cluttered private office. The strip is blank white receipt-style paper that feeds from a desk-mounted roll dispenser at the desk's edge, spills across the desk top, runs down over the front edge, and drapes across the floor in a long loose S-curve to the lower-left of the frame.
