Your manager has not asked about your project in two months. You have started to wonder what that says about you.

I used to read that kind of quiet as a sign my work was not a priority, and I would go back over my last few emails looking for the reason.

From the manager's chair it looks different. When a team is going well, nothing pulls my attention toward it. A manager of twelve is carrying about 37 topics at once, by my count, and the quiet ones sink to the bottom.

The quiet you are reading as a signal is, most likely, arithmetic.

I cannot always tell which kind of quiet I am looking at, but I start with the kinder reading.

What have you been telling yourself your manager's silence means?
