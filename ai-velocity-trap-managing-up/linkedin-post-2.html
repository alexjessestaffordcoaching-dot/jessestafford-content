Platform: LinkedIn #2 | Frame: the new limit is deciding, not building | Status: DRAFT. Not approved.

Your team can now build faster than your organization can decide what to build.

On my teams the work has moved from typing to planning, and AI makes it cheap to produce several options for what to build. Some weeks the team is stuck choosing among them, not on how to build the one it picks.

I watch the team finish work faster than the process around it can keep up, and then it sits there, stuck in the bureaucracy of it all.

I am not sure how much of the stall is the deciding and how much is the process. I do know that more options means more deciding, and that part does not get shorter.

If your team spends more of its week waiting on a decision than on the work, that is the kind of stall I help leaders sort out in coaching.
