I had the message written. The date was in the subject line, the team was copied, and I was about to send it.

Before I did, I stopped and tried to say out loud why that date actually mattered. What came out was "it is on the plan."

That is not a reason. The stakes felt obvious to me because I had been in the room where the priority got set. My team was not, so they only got the instruction.

If you can say in one sentence why the date matters, put that sentence in the message. If you cannot, that part is yours to work out before you send anything.

The last time you called something urgent, could you have said why?
