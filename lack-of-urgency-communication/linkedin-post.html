I used to think my team just did not have the same sense of urgency I did. I would flag something as a priority, and two weeks later it was moving at the same pace as everything else on the board.

They had the deadline. They did not have the reason it mattered, so they treated it like every other task already on their plate.

If a team is not treating something with the weight you think it deserves, that is not a character problem. That is a read on how much of the stakes you actually gave them.

Say the consequence instead of the label. "This needs to happen by Friday" moves nobody. "If this slips, the customer walks into a renewal conversation with the wrong numbers, and that call is mine, not yours" does.

If you have said something is urgent and watched your team treat it like everything else, that is exactly what I help leaders work through in coaching.
